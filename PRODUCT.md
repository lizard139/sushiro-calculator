# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sushiro diners using a WeChat mini program at the table or after a meal. They need fast plate counting, optional single-item entry, receipt saving, and simple spending review.

## Product Purpose

Calculate meal totals from plate colors, custom items, discounts, service fees, and people count. Success means users can settle accurately, save a receipt, and later review spending without friction.

## Brand Personality

Clear, warm, practical. The interface should feel familiar to mobile payment and receipt workflows while carrying Sushiro-inspired red accents.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards, marketing-page layouts, over-rounded nested cards, low-contrast muted text, and controls that look playful at the expense of speed.

## Design Principles

- Put totals and money values first.
- Keep repeated actions predictable and thumb-friendly.
- Use red for primary emphasis, not decoration.
- Preserve the receipt metaphor where it helps comprehension.
- Keep analysis readable before making it expressive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable system fonts, strong contrast for totals and controls, touch targets around 40 to 44 logical pixels, and restrained motion for state feedback only.
